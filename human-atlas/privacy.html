Human Atlas · Clue Club

Privacy and sources

Human Atlas is an educational collection about human origins. Clue Club is its children's section. This page describes the website's current features.

Your place in the story

Clue Club saves the discoveries you have opened, your selected adventure, your place and your preferred recorded-narration speed in this browser. These settings are not sent to the atlas publisher. They stay here until you clear them or your browser removes its site data. They do not follow you to another device.

What the website loads

The site code has no advertising, analytics trackers, child accounts, contact forms or user uploads. Stories, images, maps and recorded narration load from this website. The browser checks the website for a newer edition. Like any website, the hosting service receives connection information such as your IP address and the requested file, and may keep operational or security logs. Any sign-in used by the hosting platform is separate from Clue Club.

Listening

The children's main narrator is an AI-generated recording. Playing it does not activate a microphone or send story text to a voice service. The optional device-voice feature uses your browser or operating system's speech service; some voices may use an online service. Its privacy practices depend on your device. All story and answer words remain available as text.

Videos and external websites

Clue Club contains no embedded videos. The full atlas loads YouTube's privacy-enhanced player only after you select “Play video here.” Playing a video connects your browser to YouTube, which has its own privacy practices. Opening a publication, image source, licence or other external link also connects to that provider. These links may lead to content intended for adults.

Evidence, images and reuse

Imported database records are distinguished from curated stories. Dates, classification questions and other uncertainties remain attached to the evidence. Publication figures can show several sites or individuals: a link to a paper does not identify every image as a photograph of the selected specimen.

  • ROAD metadata: CC BY-SA 4.0, credited to the ROCEEH Out of Africa Database.
  • AADR v66.p1 metadata: CC0 1.0. This atlas distributes metadata, not raw genetic sequences.
  • Evidence photographs and figures: see each image's caption, author, source, changes and specific licence. No blanket licence is claimed over third-party material.
  • World map geometry: Natural Earth, public domain.
  • Welcome artwork: labelled AI-created illustration, not scientific evidence.
  • Recorded narration: generated using the Kokoro model; model and licence are linked in the Clue Club grown-up guide.

The atlas is a guide to published evidence, not an independent review of every underlying paper. Read the linked sources for the full methods, limits and revisions.
