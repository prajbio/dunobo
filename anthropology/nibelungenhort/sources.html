Research method and limits

Search scope

Targeted German and English searches for Nibelungenhort, Nibelungenschatz, Jacobi, Lochheim, Lôche, drilling, marble, search history, and related archaeological discoveries. Follow-up searches prioritised holding libraries, historical gazetteers, original field reports, museums and geological or heritage authorities.

Evidence rules

Separate existence of a source from truth of the source’s claim. Separate medieval literature from ancient testimony. Separate a deposit from its historical identity. Trace repeated reports to shared claim families. Distinguish a source publication date, event date, digitisation date and access date.

Inclusion and exclusions

Include sources that bear on historical plausibility, location, previous work, comparative finds or testability. Fiction, geocaching stories and sensational headlines were not treated as finds. Natural-gold extraction was retained only to prevent its confusion with archaeological searching. No treasure-presence map or numerical source score was fabricated.

Not yet completed

No systematic catalogue census; no full A/B/C textual collation; no inspection of original Jacobi records or samples; no parcel-level borehole, lidar or aerial-image analysis; no expert interviews; no contractor quotations; and no field detection trials. Full review of several technical publications remains queued. This is a public-source research dossier, not the final evidential record.

Access and dates

Relevant pages or passages were inspected where possible. Search extracts are labelled; they are weaker than complete inspection. The original DW page, some Heidelberg pages, and some GDKE documents could not be fully retrieved. Search-engine crawl dates were not treated as publication dates.

Independence and probability

Forty-two records are analytical units, not forty-two independent observations. Several concern the same manuscript tradition, settlement or Jacobi claim. All numerical success inputs from the earlier proposal remain assumptions. The calculator is for sensitivity analysis, not a forecast validated by archaeological data.

Edition corrections

This edition narrows the historical claim about Worms; separates the 2003 announcement from a dated drilling campaign; adds Sandhausen’s rescue archaeology; flags the 815 Lorsch/Hersfeld discrepancy; and identifies the reported use of dowsing. Each change has a linked evidence record.
