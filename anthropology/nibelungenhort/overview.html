How to read the labels

Established means the narrow observation is well supported — for example, that a manuscript or excavation exists. It does not mean the treasure claim is established.

Reported identifies a claim in an account without the underlying field evidence being independently inspected.

Hypothesis / inference identifies an interpretation. Planning assumption identifies a chosen input, not historical evidence.

Confidence applies to the stated observation. It is not a percentage probability of treasure.
