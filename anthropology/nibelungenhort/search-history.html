Why the searches did not settle the question

Reported cause of stopping

A depth and equipment problem is one published explanation for the Jacobi work stopping. It remains a claim to verify, not a demonstrated diagnosis of failure.

E19 An equipment limitation is a reported explanation

Limits of negative evidence

Without footprints, depth sensitivity and retained measurements, a search cannot be assigned a meaningful exclusion probability. An empty search can mean an absent target, a missed target or a poorly chosen place.

E23 Claims that all candidate places were surveyed are not auditable
E42 Negative evidence needs a defined detection probability

Source dependence around the Jacobi claim

Unlocated original field records → searcher interpretations → public statements and profiles → later summaries.

This is a provisional dependency model, not a proven publication genealogy. The reviewed accounts document circulation of the claim; they do not supply independent detections. Treat the stone, depth estimates and confidence statements as one related evidence family until original records demonstrate otherwise.
